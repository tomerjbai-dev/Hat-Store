About Brim & Crown

Brim & Crown is a small, independent hat shop in the heart of Tel Aviv. We believe the right hat does more than keep the sun off your face: it finishes an outfit and says something about the person wearing it.

On this site you can browse a selection of the hats we carry, see their prices, and find out how to reach the shop. Every hat is picked by hand for its materials and shape, and our team is always happy to help you find the right size and style.

  • Quality materialsWool felt, woven straw and soft cotton twill.
  • Personal fittingWe measure and adjust so your hat sits just right.
  • For every seasonSun hats for summer, beanies and felt for winter.
